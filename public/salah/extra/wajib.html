Witr · Eid · Jumu'ah

Wajib & Weekly Obligatory Prayers · Witr: after Isha · Eid: morning of Eid · Jumu'ah: Friday, in Dhuhr time
3WitrWajib
2EidWajib
2Jumu'ahFard

How to pray

  1. Witr: 3 rakats; sit after the 2nd (Tashahhud only), stand for the 3rd
  2. 3rd rakat: Fatiha + surah, Takbir with raised hands, then Dua Qunoot
  3. Then ruku, sajdah, Tashahhud, Durood, dua and Salam
  4. Eid: 2 rakats, no adhan or iqamah, khutbah after the prayer
  5. Extra takbirs: 3 after Thana (1st rakat), 3 after the surah (2nd)
  6. Jumu'ah: two khutbahs first, then 2 rakats fard, aloud
  7. Sunnah with Jumu'ah (Hanafi): 4 before; 4 + 2 after
  8. Jumu'ah and Eid are prayed in congregation

In brief

Witr and Eid are wajib in the Hanafi school. Jumu'ah is fard and replaces Dhuhr on Friday. Jumu'ah and Eid need a congregation.

Virtue & reward

  • “Allah is Witr and loves the Witr, so pray Witr.” (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi)
  • “The best day the sun has risen on is Friday.” (Muslim)

Tips & notes

  • Witr may wait for the last part of the night if you trust you will wake
  • On Friday: ghusl, clean clothes and early arrival increase the reward
Guard your wajib prayers: they complete the obligations that keep you steady.
