التهجد

Tahajjud

Night Prayer (Qiyam al-Layl) · After Isha and some sleep; best in the last third of the night, until Fajr
2TahajjudMinimum
8TahajjudProphet's usual
3WITRWajib

How to pray

  1. Sleep after Isha if you can, then wake and make wudu
  2. Niyyah: 2 rakats Tahajjud (nafl), then Takbir
  3. Fatiha + a surah in every rakat
  4. Prolong qiyam, ruku and sajdah as you are able
  5. Pray in pairs: Salam after every 2 rakats
  6. Make long dua in sajdah: a time of closeness
  7. Finish with Witr if you have not prayed it
  8. Then seek forgiveness (istighfar) until Fajr

In brief

Tahajjud is nafl, yet among the most emphasised acts of worship. Pray 2 to 8 rakats in pairs (the Prophet ﷺ usually prayed 8), then Witr.

Virtue & reward

  • “The best prayer after the obligatory prayers is the night prayer.” (Muslim)
  • In the last third of the night Allah calls: “Who asks Me, that I may give?” (Bukhari)

Tips & notes

  • Missed it? Pray after sunrise, before Dhuhr: written as if prayed at night (Muslim)
  • Set an early alarm: even 2 rakats count as Tahajjud.
Hold on to the night prayer: it is the habit of the righteous. (Tirmidhi)
