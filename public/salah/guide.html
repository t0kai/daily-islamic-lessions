How to make Wudu

Follow the picture: the glowing part is what you wash next
Step 1 of 10

Niyyah and Bismillah

Make the intention in your heart to pray, and say Bismillah.

!
Fard parts (Hanafi): wash the face, wash both arms to the elbows, wipe at least a quarter of the head, wash both feet to the ankles. The other steps are sunnah.
i
Do not waste water, even at a running tap. Keep the order and do each part fully so no dry spot remains.

What breaks Wudu

And what does not: then test yourself

Breaks wudu: make it again

  • Anything leaving the private parts: urine, stool, gas.
  • Blood or pus that flows out of the body.
  • Vomiting a mouthful.
  • Sleeping lying down or leaning; fainting; intoxication.
  • Laughing out loud in a prayer that has ruku and sajdah.

Does not break wudu (Hanafi)

  • Only being unsure: doubt does not break it.
  • A tiny drop of blood that does not flow.
  • Touching the private part or your spouse.
  • Eating or drinking; a short nap sitting upright.

1. Qiyam · Standing

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُআল্লাহু আকবার

Allah is the Greatest (Takbir), then Thana, Bismillah, Al-Fatiha and a surah.

Standing (Qiyam)

Face the Qiblah and stand calm and straight
FeetFace the Qiblah, about 4 fingers apart
EyesLook at the place of sajdah
Boys and menHands below the navel, right over left
Girls and womenHands on the chest
✓
Stand still and calm. Do not rock or fidget.

Ruku (bowing)

Back flat like a table
  1. Say “Allahu Akbar” and bow.
  2. Hands on knees, fingers spread, back flat, head level with the back.
  3. Say “Subhana Rabbiyal Azeem” at least 3 times.
  4. Rise saying “Sami’Allahu liman hamidah” and “Rabbana lakal hamd”.
!
Stop until every bone rests. This calm pause is tuma’ninah and it is required. Prayer is not complete without it.

Sajdah (prostration)

The closest place to Allah: ask for what you need
  1. Say “Allahu Akbar” and go down: knees, hands, then nose and forehead.
  2. Fingers together, pointing to the Qiblah, face between the hands.
  3. Say “Subhana Rabbiyal A’la” at least 3 times.
  4. Sit up, rest calmly, then do the second sajdah.
7
Seven parts touch the ground (the glowing dots): forehead with nose, both palms, both knees, toes of both feet.
M
Men: lift the elbows off the ground and keep the tummy away from the thighs.
W
Women: keep the body close and compact, elbows against the sides.

Dress code (Awrah)

The teal part must be covered
Boys and men

Navel to below the knees is the minimum. A cap is recommended.

Girls and women

Cover the whole body except face, hands and feet. Clothes not see-through or tight.

✓
Clothes, body and prayer place must be clean (pak). Use a clean mat or floor and face the Qiblah.

Essentials of Salah

Tick what you have done before you say the first Takbir

Before the prayer Conditions

  • Body clean from hadath (wudu or ghusl).
  • Body, clothes and place clean.
  • Awrah covered, Qiblah faced.
  • Prayer time has begun, niyyah made.

Inside the prayer Fard

  • Takbir Tahrimah to begin.
  • Qiyam: standing (in fard).
  • Qira’ah: at least one verse.
  • Ruku and two Sajdahs in every rakat.
  • Final sitting long enough for Tashahhud.
i
Wajib parts like Al-Fatiha and a surah must not be left. If forgotten by mistake, Sajdah Sahw fixes it. Ask your teacher.

What not to do in Salah

Each of these harms your prayer or its reward
Talking, eating or drinking
Laughing, or turning the chest from the Qiblah
Looking around or up at the sky
Playing with clothes, hair or fingers
Big unnecessary movements
Rushing without tuma’ninah
Praying when you badly need the toilet
Praying at sunrise, midday (zawal) and sunset
Walking in front of someone praying

Khushu: how to get the highest reward

Calm heart, present mind
“Worship Allah as if you see Him. If you do not see Him, He sees you.”Bukhari, Muslim

★ Pray on time

And in jamaah at the masjid when you can.

★ Know the meaning

Learn Al-Fatiha and the short surahs by heart.

★ Eyes on sajdah

Look at the place of sajdah and stay calm in every position.

★ Recite slowly

Do not rush. Give each part its right.

★ Ask in sajdah

Make dua for what you need.

★ Sunnah and dhikr

Pray the sunnah and finish with dhikr.
