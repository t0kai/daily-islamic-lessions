الظهر

Dhuhr

Noon Prayer · From the sun's zenith until Asr begins
4SunnahMuakkadah
4FARDObligatory
2SunnahMuakkadah
2NaflOptional

How to pray

  1. Make niyyah (intention) and face the Qiblah
  2. Takbir: “Allahu Akbar”, hands folded right over left
  3. Qiyam: Thana, Bismillah, Al-Fatiha + a surah
  4. Ruku: bow, “Subhana Rabbiyal Azeem” ×3
  5. Qawmah: rise, “Sami’Allahu liman hamidah”
  6. Sajdah ×2: “Subhana Rabbiyal A’la” ×3, sit between
  7. Tashahhud after 2nd rakat and at the end (+ Durood, dua)
  8. Salam to the right and left: “As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah”

Fard at a glance

4Ruku
8Sajdah
SilentRecitation

This prayer

Silent. Fatiha + a surah in 1st & 2nd rakat. Sit for Tashahhud after the 2nd. In the 3rd & 4th, Fatiha only. All silent.

Wajib

  • No separate wajib rakats
  • Wajib inside prayer: Fatiha + a surah, first sitting, calm in each posture, Salam

Nafl & notes

  • 2 nafl after the last sunnah
  • Friday: Jumu'ah replaces Dhuhr (2 fard + sunnah before & after)
Pray the 4 sunnah before Dhuhr with full focus: they open the day's midpoint.
