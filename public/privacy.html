Your trust matters

Privacy & Cookies

Short version: no ads, no selling data, and analytics only if you say yes.

Feedback messages

If you send feedback we receive your name, email, topic and message. We use them only to read your message and reply. Your email is never shown on the site and never shared. Messages are delivered by email through a mail-sending service.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you choose “Accept analytics”, we use Google Analytics to count visitors, see which pages are opened, how long people stay and which website or search brought them here. IP addresses are anonymised and advertising features are off. You can change or withdraw your choice anytime with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Stored on your device

The site saves small settings in your browser (theme, text size, surahs you marked as learned, your cookie choice). They never leave your device.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google Analytics loads only after you agree.

Advertising

This site has no advertising and does not use advertising cookies.

Children

The lessons are for children, but the feedback form is meant for parents, teachers and adults. Children should ask a parent before sending a message.

Contact

Use the feedback form. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.
