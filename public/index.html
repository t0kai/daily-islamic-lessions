For fathers

What a father owes his children in this dunya

Your children are an amanah (trust) from Allah. Here are ten duties to keep in mind, each with its source.

1

Protect them from the Fire

Care for their deen first, not only their comfort. Teach them who Allah is, and lead by example.

Quran 66:6
2

Feed and provide with halal

Give them food, clothes, a home and school from halal earnings. Neglecting those you support is a great sin.

Muslim
3

Be fair between all children

Treat sons and daughters equally in gifts, time and affection. Never favour one child openly.

Bukhari, Muslim
4

Show mercy and give love

Hug them, kiss them, speak softly, and say you love them. Rasulullah ﷺ kissed his grandsons and taught that the one who shows no mercy receives none.

Bukhari, Muslim
5

Teach them to pray

Start at 7 with kindness: take them to the masjid and pray beside them. At 10 be firmer, but never harsh.

Abu Dawud
6

Teach the Quran and good manners

Give them a Quran teacher, a good name and good adab: honesty, respect, sharing, and saying Bismillah and Salam.

Bukhari, Muslim
7

Make dua for them

A parent’s dua for a child is answered. Ask Allah for righteous children every day.

Quran 25:74 · Tirmidhi
8

Raise daughters with honour

Raise girls with care, education and dignity. Rasulullah ﷺ promised closeness to him for the one who does it well.

Muslim
9

Be the role model

Children copy what they see: pray on time, tell the truth, be gentle with their mother. You are the shepherd of your home and Allah will ask you about it.

Bukhari, Muslim
10

Spend real time with them

Play, listen, eat together and tell stories of the Prophets. Phones down when they talk to you.

Sunnah of Rasulullah ﷺ
Rasulullah ﷺ said: “Each of you is a shepherd, and each is responsible for his flock. A man is the shepherd of his family and is responsible for them.” (Bukhari, Muslim) Mothers share this trust too.
