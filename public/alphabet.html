Arabic Alphabet

28 letters. Tap a letter to see its name, how to say it in Bangla, and how it sounds with jabar, jer and pesh. Arabic starts from the right, so alif is at the top right.

Printable writing practice: every letter on its own line with a solid model, dotted letters to trace, and empty lines to write. 4 pages, A4.

Sounds are described in Bangla and English. A teacher should help you with the throat letters such as ح ع غ ق.
